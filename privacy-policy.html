01Who We Are

Data Fiduciary: Audial, a sole proprietorship operated by Rohan Saxena, at Rajgir Milap, Juhu Dhara Complex, Juhu Versova Link Road, Andheri (W), Mumbai 400053, Maharashtra, India.

Under the DPDPA, Audial is the Data Fiduciary responsible for deciding why and how your personal data is processed. You are the Data Principal — the individual to whom the personal data relates.

For any privacy-related question, request, or grievance, contact:

  • Email: support@audial.in

02Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to personal data we process when you:

  1. Visit, browse, or interact with www.audial.in;
  2. Place an Order or pay an advance for a Panel;
  3. Upload Artwork through our configurator;
  4. Use our room visualizer to plan panel placement;
  5. Contact us by email, form, or phone;
  6. Receive a Panel by delivery from our courier partner.

It does not apply to:

  • Personal data processed by other websites linked from www.audial.in, which are governed by their own privacy policies;
  • Personal data processed solely for personal or domestic purposes by you (for example, data on your own devices).

03Personal Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

3.1 Data you provide directly

  • Identity and contact details: name, email address, phone number;
  • Delivery details: shipping address;
  • Billing details: billing address, retained for GST invoicing;
  • Artwork: image files you upload for printing on your Panel;
  • Configuration data: Panel sizes, finish choices, room dimensions, panel placement choices in our room visualizer;
  • Order data: items ordered, advance and balance payment records, delivery preferences, communication history;
  • Correspondence: emails, support requests, and other communications you send us.

3.2 Data collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, and time-zone — collected by our web host for basic operational, security, and abuse-prevention purposes;
  • Session data: information about your current visit, used to keep your cart and configurator state available during the session;
  • Local browser storage: your in-progress cart, room plan, and configuration data are stored in your browser's localStorage so that they persist between page reloads. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to Audial unless you submit an Order.

3.3 Data we do not collect

In Phase 1, Audial does not use:

  • Analytics tools (such as Google Analytics);
  • Behavioural advertising or tracking pixels;
  • Third-party social media trackers;
  • Cookies for marketing or profiling.

If we add any of these in future, this Policy will be updated and you will be notified before they take effect.

05Consent and Choice

5.1 How consent is obtained

Before we collect personal data that requires your consent, we will present a clear notice describing:

  • What data is being collected;
  • The purpose for which it is being processed;
  • Your rights regarding that data;
  • How to contact our Grievance Officer.

5.2 Withdrawing consent

You may withdraw any consent at any time, with effect for future processing, by writing to support@audial.in. Withdrawal will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before your withdrawal, and may mean we can no longer provide certain services (for example, we cannot fulfil an active Order if you withdraw consent to process your delivery address).

We aim to give effect to consent withdrawals within 7 (seven) business days.

In a future version of our website, you will also be able to manage your consents through your account dashboard.

06Who We Share Your Data With

Audial does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to anyone.

We share data only with the following categories of necessary service providers, and only to the extent each requires to provide its service:

6.1 Payment collection

You pay directly by UPI to our bank account. Audial does not access, see, or store your bank details, card details, or UPI PIN.

6.2 Printing partner

Our fabric printing partners, based in India, receive:

  • Your uploaded Artwork file;
  • The size and quantity required for your Order.

Our printing partners do not receive your name, contact details, billing address, or payment information. Files are sent under contractual obligations to delete them after the print run is completed.

6.3 Courier partners

Our delivery partners (Delhivery, Shiprocket, or a similar courier, all India-based) receive:

  • Your name;
  • Your shipping address;
  • Your phone number (for delivery coordination).

They use this data solely to deliver your Panel and may retain it for their own legal compliance requirements.

6.4 Email and website infrastructure

Our email services are provided by GoDaddy and its underlying email infrastructure providers. Routine email correspondence between you and Audial passes through these systems. Our website is hosted on Netlify (United States). When you submit an Order, your details and Artwork are processed by our order system running on Netlify and stored in Google Drive (Google LLC) for our internal Order management, and a notification containing your Order details is sent to us through Resend (United States). Order requests submitted through our earlier website form were processed by Formspree (United States).

6.5 Legal and regulatory authorities

We may disclose personal data where we are legally required to do so — for example, in response to a valid order from an Indian court, a notice from a tax authority, or a request from law enforcement under applicable law.

6.6 No other sharing

We do not share your data with advertising networks, data brokers, social media platforms, or any other third party not listed above.

07Cross-Border Processing

Most of your data is processed within India. However:

  • Netlify (United States) hosts our website and runs our order system. Order submissions pass through Netlify's servers, which may be located outside India;
  • Google Drive is operated by Google LLC, and your Order details and Artwork may be stored on Google servers in countries outside India;
  • Resend (United States) delivers our internal Order notification emails, which contain your Order details;
  • GoDaddy email infrastructure may route or store email content on servers located outside India.

The DPDPA permits transfers of personal data outside India except to countries specifically restricted by the Central Government. As of the date of this Policy, the destinations above are not restricted.

If we change these providers, we will update this Policy to reflect the new processing locations and notify you.

09Your Data, Kept Safe

We take reasonable security measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit between your browser and our website and partners;
  • Access controls limiting personal data to the people who genuinely need it for your Order;
  • Use of reputable service providers (Google, Netlify, Resend) that maintain their own industry-standard security;
  • Limitation of stored data to what is needed (data minimisation);
  • Prompt deletion of data at the end of its retention period.

No security measure is perfect. While we take care, we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet or stored electronically.

10What We Do If Something Goes Wrong

If we become aware of a personal data breach that affects you:

  1. We will notify the Data Protection Board of India within the time prescribed under the DPDPA Rules (currently 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach);
  2. We will notify you, without undue delay, of:
    • The nature of the breach,
    • The categories of data affected,
    • The likely consequences,
    • The steps we are taking to address the breach,
    • The steps you can take to protect yourself;
  3. We will keep an internal record of the breach and our response.

11Cookies and Browser Storage

In Phase 1, Audial's website uses only essential cookies and browser storage:

  • Session cookies that keep your browsing session active;
  • localStorage that holds your cart, room plan, and panel configuration data on your device so they persist between page loads;
  • Security cookies set by our web host to protect against abuse and bots.

We do not use cookies for analytics, advertising, profiling, or cross-site tracking.

Because we use only essential cookies, no separate cookie consent banner is required. If we introduce non-essential cookies in future (for example, if we adopt a privacy-friendly analytics tool), we will update this Policy and present a clear consent mechanism before any non-essential cookie is set.

You can clear browser storage at any time through your browser settings. Doing so will clear your cart and room plan but will not affect Orders already submitted.

12Children

Audial's products and services are directed only at adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18.

If you are under 18, please do not place an Order or submit personal data through our website. If we discover that personal data of a person under 18 has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

If you believe a minor has provided personal data to us, please write to support@audial.in and we will take prompt action.

13Personal Data of Third Parties in Your Artwork

If your Artwork contains the image, likeness, or other personal data of any identifiable third party (including family members, friends, or public figures), you confirm under our User Content & IP Policy that you have that person's consent to upload their image. Audial processes such third-party personal data on your instructions and on the basis of your confirmed consent.

If a third party identified in your Artwork contacts Audial to exercise their rights or object to the processing, we will inform you and act in accordance with the DPDPA.

14Your Rights Under the DPDPA

As a Data Principal under the DPDPA, you have the following rights in respect of your personal data:

14.1 Right to access

You may request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and the processing activities we carry out.

14.2 Right to correction and erasure

You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data, or erase data that is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected.

Note: Some data must be retained even when erasure is requested — for example, Order records required under Indian tax law (Section 8). We will explain any such limitation when responding to your request.

14.3 Right to grievance redressal

You may raise any grievance regarding our processing of your personal data with our Grievance Officer (Section 15). If you remain dissatisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

14.4 Right to nominate

The DPDPA gives you the right to nominate another individual to exercise your rights under the Act in the event of your death or incapacity. To register a nomination, write to support@audial.in with:

  • Your full name and Order ID;
  • The full name, email, and contact details of your nominee;
  • A clear written statement of nomination, signed by you.

We will record the nomination against your customer record. You may update or withdraw the nomination at any time.

14.5 Right to withdraw consent

You may withdraw consent at any time as described in Section 5.

14.6 How to exercise your rights

To exercise any of the rights above, write to support@audial.in with:

  • Your full name;
  • The email address associated with your Order;
  • A clear description of the right you wish to exercise;
  • Any details that will help us identify the data in question (Order ID, time period, etc.).

We will acknowledge your request within 3 (three) business days and respond substantively within 15 (fifteen) business days, or sooner where reasonably possible.

We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request to prevent unauthorised access to your data.
